## The two searches

1. **Part B, a finer effort proxy for the PNW box** (Oregon, Washington, Idaho, western Montana, northern California). Candidates are road and trail line layers that could give each 250 m cell its distance to the nearest road or trail. For each candidate:
   - the publisher;
   - the licence, read at source and quoted. **Public domain or CC BY keeps the forecast data's CC BY-NC 4.0 clean. Share-alike licences don't; say so plainly.**
   - coverage of the box, including forest roads and trails;
   - currency and format;
   - size;
   - whether the attribution would need new wording on the published map.
2. **Part D, independent presences of mushroom-forming mycorrhizal genera in the PNW interior.** These must not come from the reporting platforms behind the D26 download. Examples: agency survey programmes, plot studies, herbarium surveys, published datasets. For each candidate:
   - what it is;
   - who holds it;
   - its licence or terms, quoted;
   - coverage in the interior blocks the map ranks highest (northern Rockies, Idaho Batholith, Blue Mountains);
   - the number of records, where stated;
   - its sampling design (systematic, or opportunistic like the reports);
   - whether it overlaps D26.
