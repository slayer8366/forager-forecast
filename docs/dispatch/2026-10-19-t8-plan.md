Written by the forecast planner (Alder Bearing), 2026-10-19, for the owner's rulings. Intent: RECORD.md 2026-09-28-2023. This is a plan only. Nothing is downloaded, counted or fitted under it, and nothing is built until the owner rules and gives a go.

## 0. Why T8 has this shape

- **T1's PNW result** (`docs/audits/2026-10-10-pnw-pilot-t1-report.md:288-298`):
  - 52,718 cell-weeks, 1,138 positive, 2,024 cells;
  - Brier: calendar 0.02061, weather model 0.02068;
  - pooled skill −0.0035, with an interval of −0.0066 to −0.0001;
  - verdict "worse". The weather model scored slightly worse than the calendar on held-out years.
- **T1 is the gate for all model work** (TASKS.md). T8 as written assumes a weather signal exists.
- **The owner's ruling** (Forager RECORD -1250): "A: Second, stricter PNW weather test (Recommended)". It sets:
  - a first step explaining the 2024–25 chanterelle jump;
  - effort from T6;
  - a small set of weather measures fixed in advance, with a randomisation check;
  - a bar that must beat T1's calendar on the same held-out weeks, with its interval above zero, set before any result;
  - a second "no" reported as the PNW finding.
- **This ruling departs from the spec and the task list.** SPEC.md:44 says "Trigger windows are learned from records". TASKS.md:89 says T8 will "search the windows with randomisation checks". The owner's "fixed in advance" replaces the search for this test. That needs a decision row, which is decision 4 below.
- **This is a second look at the same records.** Each further look makes a lucky pass more likely. The plan carries that by choosing nothing on held-out years, by requiring a randomisation check, and by recommending no third look without new data (decision 7).

## 1. The question

On held-out years in T1's PNW box, does recent weather improve the predicted chance that chanterelles are reported in a weather cell and week, beyond what the calendar, the place and the search effort already predict?

- **The chance** is R3's "sighting chance" (SPEC.md:66-69): the chance chanterelles are reported in a cell-week, given at least one fungal observation there that week. It is never labelled fruiting probability.
- **The box:** 42.0 to 49.5 N, 125.0 to 121.0 W (D123, DECISIONS.md:17).
- **The years:** 2015 to 2025, leave-one-year-out. A year with under 30 positives is marked uninformative and kept in the pool (D33 (2), DECISIONS.md:99). The seed is 20260918 (D31, :101).

## 2. First step: what drove the 2024–25 jump

**The jump** (T1 review, `docs/audits/2026-10-10-pnw-pilot-t1-review.md:835-837`):

| Year | Cell-weeks | Positive | Share | Skill against the calendar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 8,297 | 97 | 1.2% | +0.0080 |
| 2024 | 9,342 | 300 | 3.2% | −0.0063 |
| 2025 | 10,505 | 309 | 2.9% | −0.0080 |

**What the filed counts already show** (`docs/audits/2026-10-06-d26-build/t1_frozen_0005709_counts_by_box_year_step.csv`, the superseded T1 download, rows :117-261). These figures come from Ash's findings and were checked by Juniper:
- **PNW chanterelle records** in the box: 321, then 941, then 783. They nearly tripled.
- **All fungi** in the box: 40,744, then 47,077 (+16%), then 54,894.
- **East-box chanterelles** fell over the same years: 1,009, then 574, then 548.

So the jump is real in the raw records, it is the PNW's own, and it is far larger than the rise in observing. Whether it was a real fruiting surge or a change in who reported isn't known.

**The check: six counts from D26's zip, already downloaded, with no weather read.** These are Ash's proposal, adopted:
- J1, distinct observers;
- J2, the share of the rise from new heavy reporters;
- J3, the rise by source dataset;
- J4, the rise by week and cell;
- J5, the rate of six other autumn genera;
- J6, the share of records named to species.

**The reading rule, fixed before counting:**
- **"Reporting-led"** if over half the rise comes from new observers with 10 or more positives each, or from one dataset other than the main one, or falls in at most 3 weeks and 5 cells.
- **"Surge-consistent"** if none of those hold, distinct observers at least doubled, and at least 4 of the 6 other mycorrhizal genera rose 1.5 times or more.
- **"Undetermined"** otherwise.

**What it changes:** only the label on T8's write-up, and how the 2015–2023 line is read. It doesn't change the weeks, the measures or the bar, which are fixed first. Reporting data can't prove a surge on its own, and the write-up says so. This counting is a point of no return.

## 3. The units: "the same weeks", exactly

- **T1's own units:** 52,718 cell-weeks (T1 report :289), keyed by the `unit` array in `fits/primary_t1_1000m_calendar.npz` in the pilot data folder. That file holds unit, cell, year, y, p and land_point, read by Juniper.
- **The calendar used is T1's as stored,** not refitted: its held-out predictions `p`. T8's run refuses to start if its units or outcomes differ from T1's by one.
- **2024 and 2025 stay in the headline,** since the owner's words are "the same weeks". Dropping them after seeing T1's per-year table would be choosing weeks by the result, and it would drop 609 of 1,138 positives. A 2015–2023 line is always reported beside, with no verdict.
- **The two sensitivity runs use their own T1 units, with no verdict:**
  - D33 (3), the 5,000 m list: 56,927 units (T1 report :400-401);
  - D33 (4), the commercial-safe track (D29), read as CC0 and CC BY: 9,640 units (:364-365).

## 4. Effort

- **What R3 already removes:** only cell-weeks with at least one fungal record count, so "anyone out at all" is already conditioned away. What's left is how much searching happened.
- **The effort term:** both the weather model and the no-weather model get the log of T6's expected outings for the cell-week.
  - T6's surface is cell × year × season × day type (D106, DECISIONS.md:33). It was rebuilt byte for byte on 2026-10-19 and stored at `~/Zynergy/forecast-data/t6/` (RECORD 2026-09-28-2021).
  - **Refitted for each held-out year without that year,** so the test can't see that year's outings. The held-out year's level is interpolated from its neighbours, T6's interpolated scoring (T6 completion report :91-92, :145-146).
- **What effort can't do:** T6's surface has no weather in it, so it can't absorb people going out *because* it rained. Decision 5's turnout guard covers that.
- **T7's lesson** (Forager RECORD -1247): the access proxy, not habitat, carried T7's ranking. So the key comparison here is weather against an effort-only model, not weather against the calendar alone.

## 5. The weather measures, fixed now

Four measures, from variables already on disk in `weather/t1_pnw.npz` (soil_moisture, soil_temperature, precipitation, each as `_points` and `_values`; 4,140 days, 2014-09-01 to 2025-12-31). They are of the kinds D121 names: "recent rain, soil moisture, temperature" (DECISIONS.md:19).

| | Measure |
|---|---|
| W1 | rain summed over the last 14 days |
| W2 | rain summed over the last 56 days |
| W3 | mean soil moisture over the last 7 days |
| W4 | mean soil temperature over the last 14 days |

- **Ash proposed these four,** and I adopt them.
- **Not blind:** both Ash and I have read T1's top features (T1 report :314-315). None of the four copies a T1 window. Their reasons come from general knowledge of chanterelle fruiting, not from evidence in this repository (D4, DECISIONS.md:128: windows from elsewhere are reasons, not parameters).
- **Timing:**
  - the test is judged on windows ending the day before the week, T1's rule;
  - a "forecast" version with windows ending 7 days earlier is reported beside it. That's what a map published at the start of a week could use, given the archive's lag of about 6 days (Forager RECORD -1035; W42's data reached 2026-10-12 on 2026-10-18).
- **The randomisation check:** each year's weather is swapped whole for another year's, the same swap for every cell, 199 times (seed 20260918). The gain has to beat all but 9 of the swaps. Each measure is also checked alone and reported, as TASKS.md:90 asks ("lists each chosen window with its randomisation result").

## 6. The models

- **Main model:** a logistic model with day of year as a smooth cycle, place as smooth latitude and longitude, effort, and each weather measure as a smooth curve with 3 degrees of freedom, set now. It is chosen because there are about 1,100 positives (T1 report :289; D121 gives "about 1,200 *Cantharellus* records", :19), and because each curve can be read and checked for sense.
- **The no-weather model:** the same without W1 to W4.
- **The challenger** (D2, DECISIONS.md:130): gradient-boosted trees as in T1, with the same inputs and T1's tuning budget. It is reported, and the better model is named by Brier, as TASKS.md:90 asks.
- **Only the main model decides pass or fail.** The challenger also passes only if it clears every condition by itself.
- **By ecoregion:** leave-one-ecoregion-out over CEC level III (D125, :15). The file is on the T6b drive, `t6b/sources/cec_ecoregions_l3.zip` (35,236,046 bytes, the size in its request record). This is reported with no verdict, and is T9's input.

## 7. The bar, fixed before any result

Every interval is from 1,000 resamples of cells, seed 20260918, pooled over all held-out cell-weeks. T8 passes only if all hold for the main model, on the headline units and timing:

| | Condition |
|---|---|
| C1 | beats **T1's stored calendar** on the same weeks: Brier skill interval wholly above zero |
| C2 | beats the **no-weather model** (calendar, place, effort) the same way |
| C3 | beats the **199 shuffled-weather** versions (p ≤ 0.05) |
| C4 | the **turnout guard** (decision 5) |

**Reported beside, with no verdict:**
- the per-year table;
- the 2015–2023 line;
- forecast timing;
- ecoregions;
- D33 (3) and (4);
- each measure's shuffle;
- the challenger;
- AUC and the reliability table, as T1 reported them.

## 10. Decisions for the owner

1. **Mushrooms and weeks.** Chanterelles only, on exactly T1's weeks with 2024–25 included, and a 2015–2023 line always shown beside it. *Recommended: yes.*
2. **The 2024–25 check first,** counted from records already downloaded, read by the rule in section 2. It changes only the write-up's label. *Recommended: yes.*
3. **Effort,** from T6, rebuilt for each test year without that year, in both the weather and no-weather models. *Recommended: yes.*
4. **The four weather measures, fixed now, with no search.** This sets aside SPEC.md:44 and TASKS.md:89 for this test, recorded as a decision row. Also: judge it on the day-before timing, and report the "a week late" version that must pass before anything is shown. *Recommended: yes.*
5. **The turnout guard (C4).** The same weather must not "predict" lichen reports too. If it does, more people out in wet weather is the likelier explanation. Lichens are T6's effort benchmark (D103, D109). D109 warns of an April spike, which falls outside chanterelle season but inside the weeks, and of a limit north of 50 N, which is outside this box. **The cost:** in a strong flush, people may photograph fewer lichens, which could fail a real signal.
   - (A) required *(recommended)*. A false "yes" would put a wrong forecast in front of foragers; a false "no" costs a later retest on new data.
   - (B) reported only.
6. **The models.** A main model with readable curves decides. The tree challenger is reported, and passes only if it clears every condition itself. *Recommended: yes.*
7. **After the answer:**
   - no habitat input in T8, since T7 made no habitat claim;
   - T9 depends on T8 alone for the PNW, with no 250 m habitat shading;
   - a second "no" is the PNW finding, with no third weather test on these records without new data and your word.
   *Recommended: yes to all three.*
8. **Go-points and reviews:** one word to count, one word to fit, then two independent reviews. T8's fits wait for t6b-night to finish, or pause it. *Recommended: yes.*
