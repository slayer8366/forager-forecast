# T8b plan: a third PNW weather test, on new data

Written by the forecast planner (Alder Bearing), 2026-10-25, after the owner's "New data, then retest" (forecast
RECORD 2026-09-28-2052). Built on Juniper's survey (`docs/audits/2026-10-25-new-data-survey/report.md` on branch
`new-data-survey`, 77073f1), the T8 plan (`docs/dispatch/2026-10-19-t8-plan.md`), D133 to D137, and both T8
reviews (`docs/audits/2026-10-21-t8-fit/review-fable.md`, `review-sonnet.md`). Branch `t8b-plan`, cut from main at
98d4b33. **This is a plan, not a go.** Nothing is pulled, counted or fitted until the owner rules on section 10,
and each point of no return (-1030) is asked separately.

## 0. Why a third test, and why this shape

T1 and T8 both said no (`docs/audits/2026-10-21-pnw-finding.md`). Under D133 (7) a third test needs new data and
the owner's word. The owner gave the word for new data. The reviews named three things the T8 design could not
separate from "weather doesn't help":

- **(a) The 2021 extrapolation.** C2's loss came almost entirely from one fold: 56-day rain on the NW Olympic
  coast in weeks 45 to 49 of 2021 was 1,279 to 1,324 mm, above that fold's training maximum of 1,162 mm, and the
  splines ran on linearly past it (review-fable.md finding 1). Excluding 2021 was forbidden and stays forbidden.
- **(b) Model form.** C1's gap is mostly form: T1's calendar is a tree model that can fit season × place, and
  T8's additive model can't. The no-weather additive model alone lost to T1 by −0.00969 (review-fable.md
  finding 2).
- **(c) Coarse, shallow weather.** Rain was 0.25° ERA5 and soil was the top 7 cm only (`hourly_daily.py:33`).
  What the measures added "looks like climatology": 132 of 199 shuffled-weather models beat no-weather
  (review-fable.md finding 3). SPEC.md lists "whether 11 km weather carries enough signal in the Cascades" as
  unverified.

T8b changes exactly those three things and keeps everything else fixed: the same box, the same units, the same
bar, the same seed. A third test on the same records is also a third look, so the more freedom it takes, the
less a "yes" means. That's why the measures are fixed here, why the bar doesn't move, and why a "yes" here still
has to pass a season nobody has seen (section 7).

**What the survey ruled out,** so the plan doesn't lean on it (survey sections 2 and 3):
- No open "searched, found nothing" source exists for PNW fungi at week scale in 2015 to 2025. The fixed-plot
  chanterelle series (Andrews TP110, 1994 to 2010; Norvell, 1986 to 1997) predate every test year, and TP110's
  download wasn't reachable.
- More presences add little that is usable. Herbaria add about 18 specimens in the box for the whole period. The
  roughly 1,200 iNaturalist research-grade observations GBIF doesn't carry are outside GBIF *because of their
  licence* (all rights reserved, or share-alike / no-derivatives), so most can't be used under SPEC's licence
  rules. That figure is an estimate from two API totals, not a reconciliation.

## 1. The question

Do year-specific weather measures, at 1 km and below the surface, tell you better than the calendar in which
weeks and weather cells chanterelles get reported in T1's box, on years the model never saw, and on a season
nobody had seen when the rules were written?

## 2. The box and the units

- **T1's box, unchanged:** 42.0 to 49.5 N, 125.0 to 121.0 W (`t1_design.py:35`). The survey's "PNW" was wider
  (Idaho, northern California, most of BC). Widening would make it a different test with new units, so T8b
  doesn't (decision 1).
- **T1's units, unchanged:** the 52,718 cell-weeks of D133 (1), with 2024 and 2025 in and the 2015 to 2023 line
  beside. Chanterelles only.
- **The number stays D12's:** sighting chance, given at least one fungal report of any kind in the cell-week.

## 3. The 2024–25 jump: one more check, read by a rule fixed now

T8's jump check read "undetermined" (count-report.md). The survey found two reporting-side causes the check didn't
test (survey section 5): a change in how many records carry a location accuracy of 1,000 m or less, and a change
in the share of observers on licences that keep records off GBIF.

- **The check:** iNaturalist's own totals for *Cantharellus* in the box, by year, 2015 to 2025, (i) with and without
  a stated accuracy, (ii) with accuracy under 1,000 m, and (iii) by licence, each against the same for all fungi.
  These are counts of presences under a rule, so they sit under the counting word (section 9).
- **The reading rule:** if the share of chanterelle records surviving the 1,000 m filter, or the share on
  GBIF-eligible licences, moved by more than a third between 2023 and 2024 *and* the same share for all fungi did
  not, the jump is labelled "partly reporting". Otherwise the label stays "undetermined". Like T8's, it changes
  only the write-up's label, never the units or the bar.

## 4. Effort: unchanged

T6's surface, refitted per held-out year, in both models (D133 (3)). The survey's one new effort source,
iNaturalist "needs ID" and casual fungus observations, would change the units themselves (more cell-weeks where
someone was out), so the comparison with T1's stored calendar would no longer be on the same weeks. T8b leaves it
out (decision 3). It is the natural first input of a later, separate test on new units.

## 5. The weather measures, fixed now

Five measures, no search, windows ending the day before the week (as D133 (4)):

| | Measure | Source | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | rain, 14-day total | Daymet V4, 1 km | (c): 1 km instead of 0.25° |
| M2 | rain, 56-day total | Daymet V4, 1 km | (c), and the measure that broke in 2021 |
| M3 | soil moisture, 14-day mean, layer 2 (7 to 28 cm) | ERA5-Land, 0.1° | (c): below the top 7 cm, which follows each day's weather |
| M4 | soil temperature, 14-day mean, layer 2 (7 to 28 cm) | ERA5-Land, 0.1° | (c), same |
| M5 | dew-point depression (2 m temperature minus 2 m dew point), 7-day mean | ERA5-Land, 0.1° | how dry the air is near the ground, the closest measure to fog and drip that covers every test year in this box |

- **Clamping, against (a):** each measure is clamped to the range of its own training years in every fold. A
  held-out week wetter than any training week is treated as the wettest training week, not extrapolated past it.
  This is a rule about model form, fixed now; it doesn't drop or change any week.
- **Sources and licences,** from the register and the survey, to be re-read at source by Ash before any pull:
  Daymet V4, public domain (`DATA_REGISTER.md:10`; "Training-only test of 1 km weather in the Cascades" is already
  its registered purpose). ERA5-Land, CC BY 4.0 on the CDS entry (SPEC.md:131; the survey cites this as SPEC.md:307-316, which is past the end of the 136-line file), the same route as D24.
- **Daymet's coverage:** it covers the whole box, including southern Vancouver Island, which gridMET, AORC and
  Stage IV don't (survey 3.3, 3.6, 3.7). Its monthly latency means it can't be a live source. A "yes" here would
  still need a timely 1 km source before anything is shown (section 8).
- **Not in T8b:** snow (inferred: chanterelles in this box fruit in autumn, mostly below the snow line; not checked),
  station networks and SNOTEL (point data, and AWDB's licence isn't stated at source), satellite soil moisture,
  HRRR (its version changes across years would leak between folds, survey 3.18), GOES fog (starts 2021-10-20).

## 6. The models

- **Main model, decided by decision 4.** Recommended: **gradient-boosted trees, T1's form**, with T1's calendar
  inputs (day-of-year sine and cosine, latitude, longitude), effort, and M1 to M5. C1 then compares two models of
  the same form, so it tests weather, not form (against (b)). Hyperparameters are tuned once per fold with T1's
  budget on the real data, then held fixed for the 199 shuffles, so the shuffles test the weather, not the tuning.
- **No-weather model:** the same trees without M1 to M5.
- **Readable model, reported beside with no verdict:** T8's additive model on M1 to M5, clamped, so the curves can
  be read for sense.
- **By ecoregion:** leave-one-ecoregion-out over CEC level III, reported with no verdict. The CEC file was on the
  lost flash drive, so it's fetched again (it's on the T6b re-fetch list, 35,236,046 bytes, sha256 a99b4967…).

## 7. The bar, fixed before any result

Unchanged from T8 (D133 (5)): every interval from 1,000 resamples of cells, seed 20260918, pooled over all
held-out cell-weeks. The retrospective verdict passes only if all four hold for the main model:

| | Condition |
|---|---|
| C1 | beats T1's stored calendar: Brier skill interval wholly above zero |
| C2 | beats the no-weather model the same way |
| C3 | beats the 199 shuffled-weather versions, p ≤ 0.05 |
| C4 | the turnout guard: the weather model's gain over no-weather for lichen reports is not wholly above zero |

**And one more, before anything is shown (decision 5):**

| | Condition |
|---|---|
| C5 | **the unseen season.** The model, frozen after C1 to C4, predicts the 2026 season (ISO weeks 27 to 52 of 2026) before any 2026 chanterelle record is read, and beats T1's calendar on it: Brier skill above zero, with its interval reported. Scored once, from a GBIF download taken after 2027-02-15 so late reports are in. |

- Inferred, from the pilot's commit messages: the 2026-W41 and W42 pilot runs published predictions, and no 2026
  chanterelle outcome was read. Ash confirms this from the pilot's code before the rules are frozen. If any 2026
  outcome was read, those weeks are left out of C5 and the report says so.
- **Reported beside, with no verdict:** the per-year table (with 2021 named), the 2015 to 2023 line, a "week
  late" forecast timing, ecoregions, each measure's own shuffle, the readable model, AUC and the reliability
  table. The reviews asked for all of these; T8 missed some of them first time round (review-fable.md finding 1).

## 8. What follows from the answer

- **No (any of C1 to C4 fails):** the third PNW "no". Weather testing on public PNW records stops: no fourth test
  without a dataset independent of these observers, such as fixed plots or forays with recorded absences. The
  write-up gets one more section. Nothing is published.
- **Yes on C1 to C4, then C5 fails:** reported as a retrospective result that didn't hold on a new season. Treated
  as a no for anything shown.
- **Yes on C1 to C5:** T9 follows. Showing anything still needs the week-late timing to pass, a timely 1 km source
  standing in for Daymet and passing the same frozen check, and the owner's word (the third point of no return).

- **The order:**
  1. **Rules and code, with no real outcome read.** Decision rows, code and tests. Ash prices the pulls, re-reads

  2. **Pulls,** under one word.
  3. **Counts,** under one word (the first point of no return): the section 3 jump check and the lichen attach,

  4. **Fits,** under one word (the second point of no return): C1 to C4, never beside t6b-night. Either pause

- **Compute** (inferred from T1's logs): T1's full tree fit took 2,057 s with tuning

## 10. Decisions for the owner

1. **Where.** T1's box and units, unchanged, chanterelles only. *Recommended: yes.* Widening to Idaho, northern
   California and BC would be a new test on new units, not a retest.
2. **The weather.** Five measures fixed now (section 5): 1 km rain over 14 and 56 days, soil moisture and
   temperature at 7 to 28 cm, and how dry the air is, each clamped to its training range. *Recommended: yes.*
3. **Effort.** Unchanged from T8; iNaturalist's "needs ID" outings are left for a later test on new units.
   *Recommended: yes.*
4. **The deciding model.**
   - (A) *Recommended:* trees, the same form as T1's calendar, so C1 tests weather and not model shape. The
     readable additive model is reported beside.
   - (B) the additive model decides, as in T8, with clamping. Easier to read, but it starts behind T1's calendar
     by about −0.01 before any weather is added.
5. **The unseen season (C5).** Anything shown also needs the frozen model to beat the calendar on the 2026
   season, scored once in February 2027. *Recommended: yes.* It costs four months, and it's the only check here
   that can't have been shaped by three looks at the same years.
6. **The bar.** C1 to C4 exactly as T8, same seed. *Recommended: yes.*
7. **After a no.** A third no ends weather testing on these public records; a fourth needs independent data.
   *Recommended: yes.*
8. **Go-points.** One word to pull, one to count, one to fit, one to score C5, plus two reviews. *Recommended:
   yes.*
