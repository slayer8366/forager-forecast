## The four rules as applied (D144)

1. **Mushroom-forming genera only.** Applied through the list's `certainty`. Only "clear"
   ectomycorrhizal genera enter the main run, and "clear" requires a fruitbody type in
   `MUSHROOM_FRUITBODIES`. A genus that is ectomycorrhizal by primary lifestyle with a non-mushroom
   fruitbody is "uncertain" and is in the side run only. None reached the side run: every genus the
   side run adds is "mixed" (listed below).
2. **Family-only records in the side run only.** No family in the list is wholly ectomycorrhizal,
   so the side run took **0** family-only records. The 410 records named only to family are all
   "family not wholly in group".
3. **One presence per genus per 250 m cell.** The lowest gbifID is kept. For the licence track the
   CC0 and CC BY filter runs before thinning.
4. **A genus counts alone at 30 or more thinned presences.** Rarer genera are pooled and count as
   one unit.

**The duplicate key (V9's question).** R6's duplicate step is `duplicate_taxon_observer_cell_day`:
one record per accepted taxon, observer, 0.1° cell and day (`records/filters.py`, `r6_audit_steps`;
D27). Two genera seen by one person on one day are two records. After rule 3 they remain two
presences only if they are different genera; two records of one genus in one 250 m cell are one
presence.

## Things the count showed that bear on the fit

- **Blocks in the box.** The fit's tile table finds 26 level III blocks with study cells in the
  box (OBSERVED in `table_meta.json`, written by the fit script's `table` step). The 26th is 10.1.6
  Colorado Plateaus, with 832 study cells, 52.9% of them with shares. The T7 verify report's V12
  says "25 level III blocks" in its headline, but its own per-block list
  (`2026-10-15-t7-verify-report.md:233-246`) names 26, Colorado Plateaus included. Its "17 blocks
  at 40% or more" includes Colorado Plateaus, so D139's 18 forested blocks (17 with Canada out)
  stand as ruled, and this count reproduces them. Colorado Plateaus has no presence.
- **Effort keying.** The weather cell used for effort is the 250 m cell centre's, the same as for
  background. A record's own coordinates can fall in the neighbouring weather cell. The fit counts
  such presences.
