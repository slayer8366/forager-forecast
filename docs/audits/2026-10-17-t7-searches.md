## 1. Result in one paragraph

**Part B: usable, from public-domain federal layers, and the attribution needs one new line.**
Three federal line layers together cover the box's roads and trails, each public domain or
CC BY by its publisher's own statement: the USGS National Transportation Dataset (roads from
the Census Bureau plus a commercial vendor's roads, and trails from "multiple government
agencies"; per-state files, Idaho's shapefile 189 MB), the Forest Service's National Forest
System roads (whole-US geodatabase 239 MB, refreshed 2026-10-09) and trails (whole-US
geodatabase 119 MB, published 2025-05-14), and the Bureau of Land Management's national
public roads and managed trails (public-domain label, updated 2026-09-22). The volunteer-built
world map database is excluded: its licence is ODbL, whose share-alike clause would reach any
derived database. **Part D: no systematic independent survey of the interior was found open.**
The one body of independent presences that is open and measurable is herbarium specimens:
100,155 georeferenced Agaricomycetes specimen records in the box on the public aggregator,
28,934 of them in the interior sub-box (42 to 49 N, 120 to 111 W), from collections whose
licences are CC0, CC BY or CC BY-NC. They are a different basis of record from D26, so they do
not overlap it by construction, but they are opportunistic, old (in the interior, 1,185 from
2000 on and 285 from 2015 on), and mostly coarsely georeferenced (303 interior records at 250 m
or better; 2,620 at 1 km; 8,815 at 5 km). The Forest Service's Northwest Forest Plan strategic
surveys, the only design-based fungal survey programme found, cover the spotted-owl range west
of the interior, and their plot data are held in an unnamed "regional archive", not published.
Recommendation per part is in section 6.

## 4. How a specimen test would relate to the T7 design

For the owner, inferred throughout. The specimen records are the only open independent
presences found with coordinates in the interior. Against them, P10b's fourth condition could be
run as "the map ranks specimen cells above background cells". Three things limit what that can
show. (1) Precision: the map's cells are 250 m; only 303 interior specimen records state an
uncertainty that small, and the project's own R6 filter is "uncertainty at most 250 m"
(`docs/dispatch/2026-10-11-t7-pnw-pilot-habitat.md:120`, read). At 1 km, 2,620; a test at that
scale would have to aggregate the map to the uncertainty, which is a design choice. (2) Age:
most interior specimens predate the tree layers by forty to sixty years; stands have burned
and been logged since. (3) Effort: herbarium collecting has its own road-and-station bias, so
these presences are independent of the reporting platforms but not of access; part B's layer
would be as necessary for this test as for the main fit. The question part D was asked,
"presences that do not ride on reporting effort", is answered: there is no open set of them for
the interior. What exists is a set that does not ride on the *same* effort.

## 6. Recommendation per part

**Part B: usable.** Build the distance layer from three public-domain or CC BY federal sources
together: the USGS National Transportation Dataset's five state files (roads and agency trails;
acknowledgment "appreciated"), the Forest Service's National Forest System roads and trails
(CC BY 4.0 per the catalogue; whole-US files of 239 MB and 119 MB), and, if the dry blocks
matter, the BLM public roads and managed trails (public-domain label). All keep the CC BY-NC 4.0
data licence clean (inferred from the quoted terms; the owner's reading). The published map
needs one new attribution line naming the three publishers, in the form each asks: the USGS
acknowledged, the Forest Service and BLM named as sources. Do not use the volunteer-built world
map: ODbL share-alike. Before the download, confirm two things this report could not: that the
box's forests publish trails (the per-forest page was down), and the NTD's per-feature trail
source, since "volunteer organizations" is named as a source and USGS's `Use_Constraints: None`
is the only statement covering them.

**Part D: none usable as asked; one usable with its limits stated.** No open, systematic,
independent presence set exists for the interior blocks: the only design-based programme found
stops at the spotted owl's range and keeps its plots in an unnamed archive. Herbarium specimens
are open (CC0, CC BY and CC BY-NC per collection), independent of D26 by basis of record, and
number 28,934 Agaricomycetes records in the interior sub-box, of which 303 are stated to 250 m
