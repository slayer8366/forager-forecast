### Drift: the NBAC size-floor inference in the report

Report line 104: "the 10 ha minimum filter for agency burns was removed in 2016 ... so NBAC has no
size floor from that year." The metadata's lineage (page 4) has two entries. "September 9, 2016,
2015 NBAC (version 20160914_1144): removed the 10 ha minimum size filter for agency burns" is the
one quoted. The next entry, "December 22, 2016, 2004 to 2015 NBAC updates (version 10161128):
Ingested all agency fire polygons less than 10 ha for years 2004 to current (see September 9, 2016
below)", says the small polygons were added back to fire year 2004. So "from that year" understates
it: 2016 is the release in which the filter was dropped, and the data carry sub-10 ha agency burns
from fire year 2004 onward. The register cell ("the 10 ha minimum for agency burns was removed in
2016") and the log row ("Lineage says the 10 ha minimum filter for agency burns was removed in
2016") quote the source and are not wrong; the ambiguity is between release year and fire year.
Proposed fix: a new dated log row, not an edit, quoting the December 22, 2016 entry and stating that
the size floor is absent for fire years 2004 onward, and a superseding note on the report. Not done
by the reviewer because it touches a recorded result.
